Get a copy of your data

POPIA section 23 gives you the right to a copy of the personal information we hold about you. This downloads a machine-readable file containing your account, your customers, your products, your documents and your payment records.

Your password and any security tokens are deliberately excluded. Because the file contains your customers' contact details, treat it as confidential.
