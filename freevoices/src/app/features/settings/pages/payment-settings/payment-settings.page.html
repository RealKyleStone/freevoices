Online card payments

Add a "Pay now" button to your invoice emails and PDFs, so clients can pay by card or instant EFT. Payments are collected into your own PayFast account and invoices are marked as paid automatically. Rand invoices of R5.00 or more only.

Two things to set up in your PayFast dashboard first. Under Settings, set a Security passphrase and enter exactly the same value here — a mismatch, even a trailing space, means payments cannot be verified. Then switch on Instant Transaction Notification, or payments will arrive without your invoices ever being marked as paid.
