3. Your customers' information — we are an operator, not the responsible party

This distinction matters, so we want to be explicit about it.

When you add a customer to FreeVoices and issue them an invoice, you decide what information to collect about that person and why. For your customers' personal information, you are the responsible party and we are your operator (POPIA section 1). We process it only on your instruction, only to provide the service to you, and for no purpose of our own — we do not market to your customers, profile them, sell their information, or use it to train anything.

What that means in practice:

  • You are responsible for having a lawful basis to hold your customers' information, and for telling them how you use it.
  • If one of your customers asks you for access to, correction of, or deletion of their information, that request goes to you. We will help you action it.
  • We secure it as described in section 9, and we retain it according to your account's lifecycle and the retention schedule in section 10.
  • We are obliged under POPIA section 21 to notify you if their information is compromised while in our care.

7. Sending information outside South Africa (POPIA section 72)

Your account data and everything in it is stored in South Africa. Our servers, our database, our backups and our mail server are all hosted in South Africa, so your personal information does not leave the country in the ordinary course of using FreeVoices.

There are two exceptions:

  • Google reCAPTCHA. On the FreeVoices website, the sign-in and registration forms use Google reCAPTCHA to tell people apart from bots. Google receives your IP address and interaction data directly, and may process it outside South Africa. This is a security measure, permitted under section 72(1)(b) as necessary for the performance of our contract with you. reCAPTCHA is not used in the Android or iOS app.
  • Documents you send abroad. If you email an invoice to a customer in another country, you are sending that information there. You decide who receives your documents.

8. Direct marketing (POPIA section 69)

We will only send you electronic marketing about FreeVoices if you have ticked the separate marketing box when you registered, or turned it on later in Settings → Privacy & data. It is never bundled into accepting the Terms, and never pre-ticked. You can use FreeVoices perfectly well without it.

You can withdraw consent at any time — in Settings → Privacy & data, by using the unsubscribe link in any marketing email, or by emailing admin@madeoc.co.za. We will action it without charge.

When you opt out we delete the consent record. We keep one thing: a one-way cryptographic hash of your email address together with the date you opted out, on a suppression list. We cannot read your email address from that hash — its only purpose is to let us check a future marketing list against it, so that we can honour your opt-out without keeping your address on any marketing list. Keeping it is what makes the opt-out durable.

Service messages — email verification, password resets, receipts, security and account notices — are not marketing. We have to be able to send those to operate your account, so they are not subject to opt-out for as long as your account is open.

9. How we protect your information (POPIA section 19)

We take the following technical and organisational measures. No system is perfectly secure, but these are the ones we actually implement:

  • Encryption in transit. Traffic between your browser or app and our servers is encrypted with TLS. Our connections to the database and the mail server are encrypted too.
  • Encryption at rest. Particularly sensitive fields — your banking details, your company registration and VAT numbers, your address and telephone number, your customers' contact and address details, and the content of support messages — are individually encrypted with AES-256-GCM before being written to the database, using keys held outside it. Someone who obtained a copy of the database could not read them.
  • Passwords. Never stored. We keep only an Argon2id hash, which cannot be reversed to recover your password. We can never tell you what your password is, and we will never ask you for it.
  • Access control. Your data is scoped to your account on every request. Access to production systems is limited to those who need it to run the service.
  • Sign-in protection. Sessions expire after 12 hours of inactivity and cannot outlive 7 days. Repeated failed sign-in attempts temporarily lock the account, and sign-in is rate limited.
  • Backups. Encrypted, and automatically deleted after 30 days.
  • Restricted use of retained data. Where we must keep information after you have left, we restrict access to it and use it only for the reason it is retained.

If your personal information is accessed or acquired by an unauthorised person, we will notify you and the Information Regulator as POPIA section 22 requires, as soon as reasonably possible after we establish what happened.

10. How long we keep your information

We keep personal information only for as long as we have a lawful reason to. When a retention period ends, we delete the information or irreversibly de-identify it so that it can no longer be linked to a person.

We retain account information while your account is active. If you close your account, we delete or anonymise personal information that is no longer required within 30 days. Invoices, credit notes, quotes and payment records may be retained for legal, tax, accounting, audit, fraud-prevention or dispute-resolution purposes for 7 years from the end of the financial year in which the document was issued — because the Companies Act 71 of 2008, the Tax Administration Act 28 of 2011 and the Value-Added Tax Act 89 of 1991 require records of that kind to be kept for that period, and POPIA section 14(1)(a) permits retention where another law requires it. Backups are encrypted and automatically deleted after 30 days, which means a closed account may persist in a backup for up to 30 days after deletion; those backups are not used for any purpose other than restoring the service after a failure. Where we must retain information, we restrict access to it and use it only for the reason it is retained — retained financial records are not used for marketing, analytics, product development or profiling.

If a legal claim, a complaint to the Information Regulator, a tax audit, a fraud investigation or a law-enforcement request concerns your information, we place it under legal hold: the periods below are suspended until the matter is finally resolved, after which they resume. We will tell you if we place your information under legal hold, unless we are legally prohibited from doing so.

11. Your rights

POPIA gives you the following rights. Exercising them is free, except that we may charge the fee prescribed under PAIA for producing a copy of a record, and we will not treat you worse for using them.

The right to be told what we hold about you, and to get a copy (POPIA sections 23 and 24, read with PAIA). You may ask us to confirm, free of charge, whether we hold personal information about you, and to give you a record or a description of it, including who has had access to it. We may ask you to verify your identity first. In the app: Settings → Privacy & data → Download my data, which produces a machine-readable export of your account, your customers, your documents and your payments.

The right to have information corrected or deleted (POPIA section 24). You may ask us to correct or delete personal information about you that is inaccurate, irrelevant, excessive, out of date, incomplete, misleading or obtained unlawfully, and to destroy or delete a record we are no longer authorised to keep. Most fields you can correct yourself under Settings. If we cannot delete something because the law requires us to keep it, we will tell you which law and for how long, and we will restrict access to it in the meantime.

The right to object to processing (POPIA sections 11(3) and 11(4)). Where we rely on our legitimate interests, you may object at any time on reasonable grounds, using the prescribed Form 1. If you object we will stop that processing, unless the law requires us to continue.

The right to withdraw consent (POPIA section 11(2)). Where we rely on consent — which for us means direct marketing only — you may withdraw it at any time. Withdrawal does not affect processing that already happened lawfully, and it does not affect your account.

The right to object to direct marketing at any time (POPIA section 69). See section 8.

The right not to be subject to a decision based solely on automated processing (POPIA section 71). We do not make decisions about you by automated means alone.

The right to close your account. Settings → Privacy & data → Close my account. We delete or anonymise your personal information within 30 days, subject to the retention schedule in section 10. You can also request this without installing the app, at freevoices.co.za/legal/delete-account.

The right to complain (POPIA sections 5(h) and 74). If you are unhappy with how we handle your personal information, please contact our Information Officer first at admin@madeoc.co.za — we would like the chance to put it right. You do not have to come to us first, and you may complain directly to the Information Regulator at any time using its prescribed complaint form:
